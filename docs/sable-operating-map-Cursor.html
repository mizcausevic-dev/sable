What it is

Agency operations software: clients, projects, tasks, time, estimates, proposals, invoices, and payments inside one tenant.

Who it is for

An owner or admin running a studio, managers and contributors doing the work, and a client who should see only assigned accounts.

Why the boundary holds

Every tenant row carries organization_id. Foreign keys refuse cross-organization links. Row-level security is forced. The app never treats a submitted organization id as access.

How money is stored

Integer minor units. A line total is quantity_milli × unit_amount_minor, rounded half away from zero by the database. One currency per organization.

Report identity

Recognized revenue is the sum of issued, partial, and paid invoice totals whose issued date is in range. Outstanding is the current balance of the issued and partial invoices in that set. Collected sums payments received in range. Gross profit subtracts delivery cost from time in the same range. An empty range does not report a 0% margin.
